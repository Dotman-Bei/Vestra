# Vestra brand

## The mark

An **equipment tag**: a rounded plate with a punched eyelet in the corner, with a **V** cut through it. It is the physical object Vestra puts on every asset (the QR tag) reduced to one shape. The eyelet and V are true knockouts, so the mark works on any background.

Built on a 64-unit grid as a single even-odd path. No gradients, no effects, no fonts inside the SVGs. The wordmark is Geist SemiBold converted to outlines, kerned, with slight negative tracking.

## Files (`web/public/brand/`)

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `vestra-mark.svg` | Primary mark, blue. App icon, avatars, favicon. |
| `vestra-mark-mono.svg` | One colour, near-black. Print, documents, single-colour engraving or labels. |
| `vestra-mark-white.svg` | White, for dark or blue backgrounds. |
| `vestra-logo.svg` | Mark plus wordmark, blue mark with near-black text. Default lockup. |
| `vestra-logo-mono.svg` | Lockup in near-black. |
| `vestra-logo-white.svg` | Lockup in white, for dark backgrounds. |

The favicon is `web/app/icon.svg` (same as `vestra-mark.svg`); `web/app/apple-icon.png` is the 180 px touch icon.

## Rules

- Keep clear space around the mark of at least a quarter of its width.
- Smallest sizes: mark 16 px, lockup 20 px tall.
- Don't rotate, stretch, outline, add shadows or gradients, or recolour outside the three colours above.
- Don't separate the eyelet from the plate or use the V on its own.
- The mark is original to Vestra. The BOT Chain name and links appear in the footer as "Built on BOT Chain"; never combine the Vestra mark with BOT Chain's logo into one lockup.
